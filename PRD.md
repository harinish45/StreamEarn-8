## 1. Product Summary
StreamEarn AI is an all-in-one personal command center unifying AI technology intelligence, opportunity discovery (strict undergraduate internships and scholarships), local-first productivity workspace (Planner, Sticky Wall, Notes), and cybersecurity learning hubs.

## 2. Core Modules
* **Command Center:** Real-time multi-section dashboard with live radar metrics.
* **AI Intelligence Suite:**
  - AI Tools catalog with categorized capability taxonomy.
  - Verified, date-aware AI News stream with source citations.
  - Curated Resource Hub for learning and cybersecurity.
* **Opportunity Engine:**
  - Internship Radar with strict eligibility criteria (CSE, Cybersecurity, AI).
  - Scholarship Radar with income ceiling enforcement (<= ₹2 lakh).
  - Browser-persisted personal application pipeline (Interested → Applied → Interview → Offer).
* **Local-First Planner:**
  - Zero-dependency local productivity suite (`/planner`).
  - Weekly views, To-Do lists, Sticky Wall, and progress metrics.
